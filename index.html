Introduction

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Texas at Austin. I am passionate about learning all about technology. Specifically, I enjoy the topics of Machine Learning and Cybersecurity. I am originally from the Dallas area and have lived there my whole life. Ever since I was a child, I fell in love with mathematics and problem solving, two things that fuel my curiosity in the field even today. I am excited to begin a new chapter in my life to explore my interests in computer science and develop my professional skills.

Next semester, I hope to attend more career and networking events in hopes of landing an internship.
